### Agentic AI Grant & Funding Intelligence Assistant for Startups

**AICTE–IBM SkillsBuild Internship 2026 | Problem Statement #18**

## 📌 Overview

**FundFlow AI** is an AI-powered Grant and Funding Intelligence Assistant designed to help startups discover, evaluate, and prepare for relevant funding opportunities.

Funding information is often scattered across different platforms and documents, making it difficult for startups to identify opportunities that match their **startup stage, domain, location, funding requirements, and eligibility criteria**.

FundFlow AI combines **IBM Granite, Agentic AI, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** to transform funding information into personalized and explainable funding recommendations.

> **Right Opportunities. Smarter Decisions. Stronger Startups.**

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

### Problem Statement #18 — AI Grant and Funding Finder for Startups

Startups face several challenges when searching for funding:

- Funding opportunities are distributed across multiple platforms.
- Eligibility criteria and application requirements vary between programs.
- Deadlines and funding information may change frequently.
- Manually comparing opportunities with a startup profile is time-consuming.
- Funding information may exist in unstructured documents and different formats.
- Preparing customized funding applications requires significant effort.
- AI-generated application content requires human validation.
- The system must respect legal boundaries and must not access restricted portals or submit applications automatically.

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

FundFlow AI provides an intelligent funding discovery workflow that:

1. Collects the startup's profile and funding requirements.
2. Retrieves relevant funding opportunities.
3. Analyzes eligibility requirements.
4. Matches and ranks opportunities according to the startup profile.
5. Grounds important information using retrieved sources.
6. Generates customized draft application content.
7. Allows users to review and validate information before applying.
